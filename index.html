Buying guides

WheeBuy shoe finds

When you compare sneaker listings, save the seller’s item ID, selected colorway and size chart. Measure your foot from heel to toe in centimeters and compare it with the seller’s chart. If the chart lists insole length, ask how it was measured before using it to choose a size.

For warehouse shoe photos, request a side view of each shoe and a clear outsole photo. Check for mismatched sizes, surface marks and damage around the toe or heel. Record whether the shoe box is included if it matters for delivery or storage.

WheeBuy clothing size guide

Lay a garment that fits you flat. For a hoodie or jacket, measure chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and back length. For trousers, compare the laid-flat waistband, rise and inseam. Use the same units and measurement method when you read the garment size chart.

Check the fabric description and compare close-ups of the cuffs, hem and zipper. For matching sets, check the top and bottom measurements separately. For jerseys, confirm whether names or numbers are part of the selected variant.

WheeBuy QC photos

Use this quality control checklist when warehouse photos are available. Match the item ID and variant, verify the size tag, then zoom in on seams, fasteners and visible marks. Ask for a daylight color photo or a tape-measure photo if the supplied views leave a specific question unanswered.

For a cap, inspect the brim, adjustment strap and inside label. For a bag or bracelet, check the clasp, hardware and any surface scratches. Save the images and check the seller’s return window before approving shipment.

WheeBuy shipping guide

Build a landed cost estimate from the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees and international shipping. Ask for packed parcel dimensions and weight. Bulky packaging can change a volumetric weight quote even when the item itself is light.

For delivery to the US, UK, EU or Canada, check route availability by postal code and ask which fees are included in the quote. If you consolidate items, request a fresh estimate for the combined parcel and confirm the carrier’s tracking and delivery terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is a WheeBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research index that groups shopping links by category. Use it to build a shortlist, then confirm the exact item, size and seller on the live listing.

Where do the category links go?

The ten category links open Hipobuyfind listings. Check the destination for current product information and availability.

Can QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Warehouse photos can show visible condition and help you check the selected variant. Use a reliable authentication service if brand authenticity matters to your purchase.

Why can the final shipping quote change?

The carrier may charge by the parcel's dimensions or its actual weight. Recheck the packed size, destination and service terms before paying for shipping.
